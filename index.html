What We Do

Four things, done to a standard.

Low voltage work is invisible when it's right and miserable when it isn't. We scope the site, run the cable properly, label every drop and hand you a system you can actually understand.

Wired Ethernet

Cat6 and Cat6A runs, wall plates, keystones and patch panels — terminated, tested and labelled. Hardline is still the fastest, quietest, most reliable path between two points, and it never argues with your microwave.

  • New builds, renovations & retrofits
  • Patch panel & rack termination
  • PoE runs for cameras and access points
  • Outbuilding & garage links

Wi-Fi Systems

Coverage designed around your actual floor plan, not guesswork. Access points placed where they'll do work, wired back to a switch so every unit has real bandwidth behind it — one seamless network, no dead zones, no extenders daisy-chained down a hallway.

  • Site survey & coverage planning
  • Ceiling & wall mounted access points
  • Guest and staff network separation
  • Multi-storey, shop and warehouse coverage

Security Cameras

Interior and exterior IP cameras with local recording — footage lives on site, on hardware you own. Angles chosen to cover approaches and entry points, cabling concealed, and nothing that stops working the day a subscription lapses.

  • Local recording — no cloud subscription
  • Night vision & weather-rated exterior units
  • Soffit, wall and ceiling mounting
  • Remote viewing on phone or desktop

Doorbell Cameras

See and speak to whoever is at the door, from anywhere. Wired for power so there's no battery to pull down and recharge every few weeks, and integrated into the same system as the rest of your cameras.

  • Hardwired — no battery swaps
  • Two-way audio & motion alerts
  • Package and porch coverage
  • Shares one app with your cameras

The Difference

No licence fees.
No subscriptions.
No hostage situation.

A lot of camera and network gear on the market is cheap up front because the real cost arrives monthly — a fee to keep your own footage, a fee to unlock a feature, a fee that climbs every renewal. Stop paying and the hardware you bought turns into a paperweight.

We build with equipment that carries no licensing or recurring software cost. Your recordings stay on your property. Your network runs whether or not anyone remembers a credit card expiry date. You paid for it once, and it's yours.
